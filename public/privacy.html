Privacy policy

For the apps TapDose, TapWeigh, TapLift and MedTap, made by the same developer from the same code. Last updated 24 October 2026.

In short: everything you record stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We (the developer) never receive your information, and we don't sell or share it with anyone.

What the apps record

Only what you enter or choose to import. Depending on the app, that can include:

  • TapDose and MedTap: medicines, doses, schedules, stock, appointments and lab results you type in.
  • TapWeigh: weigh-ins, body measurements, goals, progress photos and food.
  • TapLift: workouts, routines, personal bests, weigh-ins and body composition.
  • All of them: check-ins (how you feel, sleep, things you noticed), blood pressure and heart rate readings, and NFC tags you link.

This is stored in the app's own storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to any server.

Health Connect

If you turn on Health data in Settings, the app reads from Health Connect only the record types you allow, such as steps, sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability, weight, body fat, lean mass, blood pressure and exercise sessions. Each app asks only for the types it uses. The app never writes to Health Connect.

What it reads is used on your phone to show your own charts and patterns (for example, sleep against your check-ins). It is not sent anywhere, not used for advertising, not sold, and not used to decide anything about you. You can stop access at any time in Health Connect's settings.

The use of information received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

When something does leave your phone

Only when you ask for it:

  • Food barcodes (TapWeigh, MedTap): scanning a barcode sends that barcode number, and nothing else, to Open Food Facts, a free public food database, to look up the product. Nutrition labels you photograph are read on the phone and the photo isn't kept.
  • Backups and exports: backup files and spreadsheets go where you choose to save or send them. Backup files are encrypted.
  • Family sync (optional): shares your records between your own and your family's phones. Everything is encrypted on the phone with a key only those phones have; any server in between sees only scrambled data.
  • Update check (versions downloaded from GitHub only): asks GitHub once a day whether a newer version exists. Nothing about you is sent. Versions from Google Play don't do this; Play updates them.

Permissions

  • NFC: to read and write the tags you stick on things, so one tap logs or opens what the tag is for.
  • Notifications and alarms: for reminders and check-ins you turn on.
  • Camera (TapDose, TapWeigh, MedTap): to scan barcodes and labels while the camera view is open. Nothing is recorded or kept.
  • Health Connect: read only, as above, and only if you turn it on.

Deleting your information

Settings → About → Erase all data deletes every record from the app. Uninstalling the app deletes them too. Because nothing is stored with us, there is nothing for us to delete.

Children

The apps are for adults and aren't directed at children.

Not medical advice

The apps are personal record-keeping tools. They don't give medical, dietary or training advice.

Changes and contact
