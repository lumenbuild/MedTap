# Google Play listings

Text for each app's store listing (Play Console → the app → Grow → Store presence → Main store listing). Graphics are in
each app's folder here: `icon-512.png`, `feature-graphic.png` and `screenshots/1.jpg` to `6.jpg` (phone, 1080×1920).
Regenerate them with `scripts/store/make-store-graphics.mjs` (how to run it is at its top) when the app changes enough
that the screenshots no longer match it.

Package names are permanent once uploaded: `app.tapdose.tracker`, `app.tapweigh.tracker`, `app.taplift.tracker`.

## TapDose

**App name** (30 max): TapDose: Medicine Tracker

**Short description** (80 max): Tap a tag on your medicine to log the dose. See what's due and how you feel.

**Category:** Medical · **Contact website:** https://lumenbuild.github.io/MedTap/tapdose/ ·
**Privacy policy:** https://lumenbuild.github.io/MedTap/tapdose/privacy.html

**Full description:**

Stick a small NFC tag on each medicine. Tap your phone on it and the dose is logged, with the time. No unlocking, no hunting through menus, no wondering later whether you took it.

TAP TO LOG
• One tap on the tag opens straight to that medicine and logs the dose, even when the app is closed.
• It shows what you've already had today, so a second tap doesn't mean a double dose.
• No tags yet? Log by hand from Today. Any NTAG213 or NTAG215 sticker works.

KNOW WHAT'S NEXT
• Today shows the next dose with a countdown, and what's done.
• Reminders with Log it, In 15 min and Skip, right on the notification.
• Set times, every few hours, as needed with your own limits, on-and-off courses and planned changes.
• Stock counts and when you'll run out, with repeats and script expiry.

SEE WHAT WORKS
• Quick check-ins on mood, focus, energy and sleep, set against your doses.
• Effect window: when your medicine usually starts working and when it wears off.
• Days on and off, caffeine and alcohol against sleep, and heart rate after a dose (from Health Connect).
• A one-page summary since your last appointment, and a doctor report to print or send.

YOURS ALONE
• Everything stays on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking.
• App lock with a PIN, nicknames for medicines, and discreet reminders.
• Encrypted backups, and optional family sync encrypted end to end.
• Track for family members too, each with their own medicines and tags.

TapDose is a personal record-keeping tool. It isn't a medical device and doesn't give medical advice: always follow your prescriber's instructions.

## TapWeigh

**App name** (30 max): TapWeigh: Weight & Food Log

**Short description** (80 max): Tap a tag on your scales to log your weight. See the trend, your goal and food.

**Category:** Health & Fitness · **Contact website:** https://lumenbuild.github.io/MedTap/tapweigh/ ·
**Privacy policy:** https://lumenbuild.github.io/MedTap/tapweigh/privacy.html

**Full description:**

Stick a small NFC tag on your scales. Step off, tap your phone on it, and your weigh-in is logged. Then see where your weight is really heading, past the daily ups and downs.

TAP TO LOG
• A tag on the scales opens Log weight in one tap.
• A tag on your tape measure opens your measurements.
• A tag on a food or drink you have often (a protein shake, a can) logs one serving.
• No tags yet? Everything can be logged by hand. Any NTAG213 or NTAG215 sticker works.

SEE WHERE IT'S HEADING
• A trend line that smooths out water and food swings.
• Set a goal and see when you'll likely reach it, with a range, updated every time you log.
• Plateaus pointed out, with what was different while it was flat.
• Waist, hips and more, body fat and lean mass, progress photos (kept on your phone).

FOOD WITHOUT THE FUSS
• Scan a barcode with the live camera: energy, protein, carbs, fat and the micros on the label.
• Photo a nutrition panel for anything the database doesn't have.
• Meals you make, by hand portions: a palm of protein, a fist of veg. No weighing.
• Batch recipes: enter once, log a serve each time.

FIND WHAT HELPS
• Daily habits: food, steps, sleep and heart rate over time, against your own targets.
• What goes with what: caffeine and alcohol against sleep, busier weeks against weight change.
• Evening check-ins on energy, hunger and sleep, in a few taps.

YOURS ALONE
• Everything stays on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking.
• Weight, body fat and steps can come in from Health Connect (read only).
• Encrypted backups, an app lock, and family members with their own records.

TapWeigh is a personal record-keeping tool. It doesn't give medical or dietary advice.

## TapLift

**App name** (30 max): TapLift: Gym Workout Log

**Short description** (80 max): Tap a tag on a machine to log your sets, with a target ready from last time.

**Category:** Health & Fitness · **Contact website:** https://lumenbuild.github.io/MedTap/taplift/ ·
**Privacy policy:** https://lumenbuild.github.io/MedTap/taplift/privacy.html

**Full description:**

Stick small NFC tags on your gym bag and the machines you use most. Tap one and the workout starts, or that exercise is added, laid out at what to aim for today.

TAP TO LOG
• A tag on your gym bag starts your routine.
• A tag on a machine or rack adds that exercise, with today's target from your last sessions.
• A tag on the treadmill or bike times your cardio: tap at the start and again at the end.
• A tag on your water bottle or shaker adds that much water or protein.
• No tags yet? Start a workout by hand. Any NTAG213 or NTAG215 sticker works (use on-metal tags on equipment).

KNOW WHAT TO LIFT
• Targets from your own history: up a step when every set hit its reps, hold when it didn't.
• Ready to train: today's sleep, resting heart rate and HRV against your own normal (from Health Connect), plus soreness.
• Rest timer that starts itself, longer after heavy sets.
• Personal bests, estimated one-rep max, and a lighter week when a lift stalls.

SEE IT ADD UP
• Sessions each week, streaks and goals, with likely dates for strength goals.
• Sets per muscle, push against pull.
• Weight, body fat and lean mass together, and how much of any weight lost was lean mass.
• Protein and water by day, against your targets.

YOURS ALONE
• Everything stays on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking.
• Cardio sessions, steps and sleep can come in from Health Connect (read only).
• Encrypted backups, an app lock, and family members with their own records.

TapLift is a personal record-keeping tool. It isn't a training programme and doesn't give health advice.
